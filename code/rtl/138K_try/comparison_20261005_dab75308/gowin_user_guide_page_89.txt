6 Output Files 
In the process of FPGA design, in addition to bitstream file, Gowin 
Software can also generate multiple reports for reference by different 
operating parameters. They are synthesis, place & route, ports, timing and 
power reports. In addition, you can right-click Place & Route to modify the 
configuration to generate pins constraints, timing simulation files, etc. 
6.1 Synthesis Report 
GowinSynthesis will generate synthesis reports and netlist files after 
synthesis. 
The report named *_syn.rpt.html is generated, including Synthesis 
Message, Synthesis Details, Resource, and Timing, as shown in Figure
